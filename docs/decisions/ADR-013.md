# ADR-013: Unattended AI Verification and Release Eligibility

**Status:** Accepted 2026-10-25, implementing the user's approval of PRD D-22. Product requirements remain in [PRD #1](../01-PRD.md); process in [#0](../00-SDD-Planning-Workflow.md); executable evidence rules in [#6](../06-verification-plan.md).

**Context:** Mandatory multilingual human review, manual browser matrices, physical-device access and visual sign-offs prevent an AI executor from completing development and release qualification unattended. Repeated local waivers leave the central requirements binding. An execution-only single-milestone stop also forces routine handoffs even within an authorized roadmap train.

**Decision:** AI may plan, review, lock, execute and close increments and advance to the next eligible increment within an authorized train. Required acceptance is established by unattended automated checks, pinned AI grading/reference review/adjudication and recorded semantic/visual review. Required browser assurance covers pinned Playwright Chromium desktop/narrow viewports. Human verification/sign-off and additional engines, browser versions, physical devices and AT observations are optional and nonblocking. Intentional baseline changes require actual image review, rationale and independent structural checks; blanket regeneration is not review.

Live qualification remains required where allocated, separate from offline feedback. Standing authorization names allowed profiles/environments, finite dispatch/spend limits and shared monetary scope. AI may run within those limits without per-run human approval, reuse unaffected evidence after impact assessment and consolidate overlapping assertions into one sufficient run. Missing credentials/budgets, unresolved grades/critical findings and failed required checks remain blockers. AI determines RG-001–008 eligibility; deployment additionally requires an authorized target and rollout/rollback limits.

**Consequences:** No mandatory reviewer or browser-device staffing remains. Cross-platform and human language assurance are narrower, and AI reviewers can share errors; reports retain model/role/run identities, calibration, findings and limitations. Quality/performance thresholds, corpus/workload counts, authentication, durable accounting, migrations, privacy and monetary admission remain binding. Required checks and runners still need implementation and execution; a policy change is not a pass. Completed milestone records/locks remain historical; D-21-affected evidence is not recertified. Future packages follow the amended owners; unfinished packages need reviewed impact updates before resumption.
